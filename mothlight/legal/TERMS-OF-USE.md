## 1. The service

Mothlight lets you write down a worry, "release" it as an animated moth, and
receive a certificate when it symbolically "dissolves" at the next sunrise it
encounters along Earth's day/night boundary. The text you write is discarded
at the end of the release ritual and is never stored beyond that point — see
our [Privacy Policy] for the full detail.

Mothlight is offered for entertainment and personal reflection purposes only.
It is **not** a medical device, and it does not provide therapy, counseling,
or any form of medical or mental-health treatment or advice. If you are in
crisis or need support, please contact a licensed professional or your local
emergency services.

## 2. Free features and Premium subscription

The core ritual — writing, releasing, and receiving a certificate — is free to
use, limited to one release per night. Mothlight Premium removes that limit
and unlocks additional moth species and a longer breathing ritual. Premium is
offered as:

- **Weekly** subscription, billed automatically every week until cancelled.
- **Yearly** subscription, which may include a free trial period (currently
  3 days) before the first annual charge, billed automatically every year

- **Lifetime**, a one-time purchase that is not a subscription and does not
  renew or require cancellation.

Prices are shown in the purchase screen in your local currency before you
buy, and are also listed on the App Store/Play Store product page.
